### **"CAD-2"**
made few changes to plate. made a hole of rotarty encoder as i forgot to make one earlier and also made the bottom part of the case. 

### "CAD ALMOST OVER"
in this lapse i completed the plate. made another thing below the plate to use it as support between pcb and plate. took out 1mm from sides of space which  was for the switches to account for printing tolerance. for the stablizers. i just removed a rectangle instead of making it so it perfectly fits. i also tried to make small legs at the bottom so it dont just slides

### "Finshing touch"
in this lapse i changed the colour of the mx cherry to black so  it matches the colour therme. also i just jot rid of the legs which i couldnt make using revole. i even watched some tutorials. instead of that i am going with curved botttom so the front part is lower compared to back side but in this lapse i did the complete opposite and made front part higher. also made some really cool colour theme with purple colour.

**"github"**
it is not what it sounds like. i  started the lapse with intention to start making the repo but in between i realised that i didnt add any silkscreen in the pcb and i also rounded the corners of the pcb which i intially forgoted.

### "assemblly"
i fixed the slope so now it is lower at front side. i forgot the main thing about integrated plate mount 'the screws'. yeah, i totally forgot the screw. i added the hole for heat insert. i am going to use m2 screws for it. i assembled everything in assembly. for the heat insert i used this formula
